# Hosted Checkout

The hosted payment page at `checkout.payment.et`. You POST a payment
to the API, get back a `redirectUrl`, and send the customer there. The
page handles the entire payment flow — method selection, OTP entry,
confirmation, error states — and bounces the customer back to your
`success_url` or `cancel_url` when finished.
